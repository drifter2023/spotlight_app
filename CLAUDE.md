# 聚焦阅读 MVP — rules for Claude Code

Read `README.md` (in the handoff folder) before any work. It is the implementation spec.

## Scope guard
- Build only: Home (continue reading + the 札记 line + shelf + import), Reader 4a, word drawer, 9px footnotes, settings sheet, open-book transition.
- Added by the owner after the MVP (TASKS.md → M5): 认识 (formerly 撤回, renamed 2026-11-11: marks a mistaken lookup as a known word) in the word drawer; a grammar droplet after each sentence in the focus band that opens a grammar drawer, analysed by DeepSeek (TASKS.md → M6, `docs/plans/2026-09-29-deepseek-grammar.md`); since 2026-09-30 the grammar drawer shows the sentence's translation on top (owner request, prompt v3). Drawers open only from sharp text (the focus band, or every line while 聚焦模糊 is off) below the title area. Predicted words (TASKS.md → M7, `docs/plans/2026-09-30-predicted-words.md`): words the reader probably does not know get their footnote in advance, learned on the device from lookups, 认识 (撤回), reading and a one-minute calibration test on Home. A 纸白 / 夜读 theme capsule on Home switches the whole app (1c colours in the reader, 7b colours on Home; layout unchanged); it is the only switch. 夜读 1c (TASKS.md → M12, `docs/handoff/2026-10-20/NIGHT_MODE_1c.md`, decisions in `docs/plans/2026-10-20-night-guide.md`): at night the reader colours its sharp lines. 带读 (auto guided reading) was removed by the owner on 2026-11-11: its bar, its play button and its speed setting are gone, and the page has no persistent control. Owner decisions of 2026-11-11 (review `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-experience-review.md`, O2–O6): a one-line first-run hint under the eyebrow, shown once; the top bar and capsule fade after 5 s idle; small text (eyebrow, footnotes) meets 4.5 : 1; the drawer's second button reads 认识; one night accent (#ffb4b4) for Home and the reader. ‹ 书架 shows and hides with the top bar and capsule; nothing stays on the page while reading (owner decision 2026-11-11, replacing the 2026-10-26 always-visible ‹ 书架). Footnotes: opening a word footnotes it at once, everywhere in the book, with no 保存 needed, except the ~200 most frequent lemmas (I, you, be, to, so…; `COMMON_RANK` in `src/core/annot/footnotes.ts`), whose drawer opens but which get no footnote unless 保存 is pressed (owner decision 2026-11-11, replacing the 2026-10-26 footnote-after-保存 rule). A 聚焦模糊 switch in the reader settings turns the focus blur off (TASKS.md → 30): the whole page is sharp and any word below the title area opens its drawer. A tap in the title area at the top of the reader (down to just below the eyebrow) shows or hides the top bar (‹ 书架) and capsule (目录 · Aa), even on a word (TASKS.md → M11, `docs/plans/2026-10-14-select-to-ask.md`; owner decision 2026-10-15); since 2026-10-20 so does a tap on blank space or the page edge that opens nothing, while a tap on text (blurred or sharp) only hides them (owner request). The Agent drawer (TASKS.md → M8, `docs/plans/2026-10-03-agent-drawer.md`, design `Agent 抽屉 · 聚焦阅读 v3`): a left swipe pushes the page aside into a card and reveals a per-book chat with a reading companion (“旁注”) backed by DeepSeek; sessions are stored on the device, each question with its place in the book, and are listed and deleted on Home's 札记 page. Once a question is sent, the 当前句 picker (or the 问旁注 selection) leaves the slot above the input until the drawer is reopened, and follow-ups are about the same text (owner request 2026-11-11). Link import (TASKS.md → M9, `docs/plans/2026-10-12-link-import.md`): Import also takes a pasted link; our own Cloudflare Worker fetches the page, the app extracts the article (Readability) and stores it as a book (web build first, iOS later). 札记 (TASKS.md → M13, `docs/plans/2026-11-11-zhaji.md`; owner decision 2026-11-11): once the reader has asked 旁注 something, Home shows one line, 札记, under the continue block; it opens a page with two views. 对话 lists the conversations of every book (open in the reader, swipe to delete). 札记 is a journal: for each book and each reading day (04:00 to 04:00), DeepSeek turns that day's questions and answers into at most five short notes, each with the quoted sentence, its chapter and the original question; the quote and the place are copied from the stored conversation, never written by the model. The first 札记 is made by a tap on 整理; after that tap, a day that has ended is summarised when Home is opened, unless the reader turns 自动整理 off. Notes are re-read, not tested: no counts, streaks, grades, due dates or hidden answers. Where these lines disagree with README's out-of-scope list (night mode, LLM, archivist agent, review of what was learned), these lines win.
- Do NOT build:
  - tabs 书房 / 生词 / 我 or a tab bar (the 札记 page's 札记 / 对话 switch is a filter inside one page, like 在读 / 读完);
  - today stats or streak, on Home or in 札记 (no counts of questions, notes, words, minutes or days);
  - review cards or spaced repetition (札记 is a journal to re-read: no flip or hidden answer, no 记得 / 不记得, no per-note schedule, no queue);
  - pronunciation / TTS, or the Examples button;
  - any LLM or agent other than the DeepSeek grammar analysis, the DeepSeek Agent drawer and the DeepSeek 札记 summary (no tools; no memory across books: a 札记 request holds one book's conversations of one day, and 旁注 is never given a 札记; no background runs: a 札记 is made only while Home is on screen, never by a timer, a service worker or a notification); sync, push, local notifications, accounts, analytics SDKs;
  - long-press sentence translation (the only sentence translation is the one on top of the grammar drawer).
  - 带读 / auto guided reading, or any play, step-through or auto-scroll mode (removed by the owner 2026-11-11).
- **Zero network requests at runtime**, with two exceptions: `https://api.deepseek.com`, called with the user's own key only (a) after a tap on a grammar droplet, (b) after the reader sends a question in the Agent drawer, or (c) for 札记: after a tap on 整理, or, once the reader has made a first 札记 by that tap and has not turned 自动整理 off, when Home is opened and a reading day with conversations has ended (at most 3 requests per opening, the last 7 days only, never without a saved key, so a fresh install still requests nothing); and our own Worker's `POST /api/fetch`, only after the reader submits a link in Import. The Worker fetches public http(s) pages for its own origin only and never logs or stores the URL. Fonts, dictionary and books are all bundled; lookups, footnotes and everything else stay offline.
- If a design doc and the README disagree, the README wins. If the README is silent, follow `MVP 设计与范围` Part B3 (Reader 4a) exactly. The 2026-10-14 changes are in `docs/handoff/2026-10-14/CHANGES_2026-10-14.md`, with the owner's decisions in `docs/plans/2026-10-14-select-to-ask.md`.

## Identity
- App display name: **Spotlight**. Bundle ID: `com.lin.spotlight`.
- Phonetics: American IPA (ipa-dict `en_US`).

## Stack
- React + TypeScript (strict) + Vite.
- Targets: **iOS (Capacitor) and a mobile web app.** No Android — this overrides any mention of Android in the README or design docs.
- SQLite, two files: `user.db` (read/write) and a read-only `dict.db`. `user.db` uses the Capacitor plugin on iOS and sql.js + IndexedDB on the web. `dict.db` is read into sql.js on both, so lookups are synchronous (see `docs/plans/2026-09-26-m2-m4-notes.md`).
- Haptics are iOS-only; iOS Safari has no vibration API, so the web build skips them.
- EPUB and TXT parsing in a Web Worker (JSZip + linkedom, since `DOMParser` is not available in workers).
- Storybook (dev only, `npm run storybook`): stories next to the components; fixtures in `src/views/_stories/`. Update a component's stories when you change it (DEVELOPING.md → Component stories).

## Architecture rules
- Keep domain modules framework-free TS under `src/core/`:
  - `library`, `ingest`, `reader`, `dict`, `annot`, `prefs`, `grammar`, `ai`, `vocab`, `agent`, `notes`.
  - React views live under `src/views/` and only call module APIs.
- The reader engine owns the scroll container and writes line styles directly (`style.filter`, `style.opacity`). Never re-render body-text lines through React on scroll.
- Build the line table once per layout. Do not call `getBoundingClientRect` per line per frame.
- One easing curve for the whole app: `cubic-bezier(.42,0,.58,1)`.
- Footnotes are `position:absolute` children of the word span. Line height must never change.

## Quality bar
- Every task in `TASKS.md` cites an acceptance ID (R-xx / H-xx / S-xx). Write a test or a measurable check for it.
- Performance: 60fps reader scroll on a mid-range device. Tap-to-drawer ≤ 100ms. A 300-page book is readable within 3s of import.
- Minimum hit target 44px. Small text contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
